## About

Train a small neural network on a small, noisy 2-D classification problem and
**see overfitting with your own eyes**: a BIG model that memorises the training
points, a SMALL model that generalises better, and a REGularised model in
between. You then design and run your own experiments with network size and
the regularisation strength `alpha`, and finish with reflection questions on
bias, variance and generalisation. About 60 minutes; no GPU needed.
