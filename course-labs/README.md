# Course Labs

Notebooks for the end-of-day **Course Lab** sessions (17:00–18:00 EAT on
Days 1–4; Day 5's Course 7 lab runs 14:30–15:30), organised by day. Click an
**Open in Colab** badge to launch a notebook directly in Google Colab — no
local setup required.

Days 1 to 4 are here now; the other days land as the week goes on. Days 1–4 run on
CPU with no setup; the Day 5 lab needs a Colab **T4 GPU** and access to Gemma 3
1B and 4B — the setup steps arrive with the notebook in day5/.
