## About

Turn a handful of words into small numeric vectors ("embeddings"), compute the
distances between them, and ask whether tokens that are *close* in vector space
are also *similar* in meaning — the idea of **meaning as geometry**. You
implement `euclidean_distance`, a nearest-neighbour search, and
`cosine_similarity` yourself. About 60 minutes; no GPU needed.
